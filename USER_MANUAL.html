📖What is this System?▼

Misbah ul Hoda — Arbaeen 2026 is a complete digital management platform for pilgrims traveling to Karbala. Everything previously done on paper, WhatsApp groups, or phone calls is now in one place.

👥User Roles▼
🕌
Pilgrim — Registers for Arbaeen, submits travel details, tracks application status, views hotel & room, sees Majalis schedule, tracks Karwan live on map. Receives automatic notifications for all important events.
🤝
Volunteer — Logs in with credentials provided by admin. Views assigned tasks with priority and deadline, checks in/out for attendance, coordinates pilgrim support on the ground. Receives WhatsApp and app notifications for emergency assignments.
⚙️
Admin — Full management access. Approves/rejects pilgrims, allocates rooms and buses, manages Majalis program, sets up accommodation, coordinates airport transfers, creates tours, sends notifications and announcements.
👑
Super Admin — All admin permissions plus: user creation, role management, finance, international payments, system reports. Full access to every feature.
🕌 Pilgrim Guide
📝Register & Log InStep 1▼

  1. Go to the website and click "Pilgrim Portal"

  2. Click "Register" — enter your full name, email, and a password

  3. Log in with your new account — you will land on your personal Pilgrim Dashboard

👤Complete Your ProfileStep 2▼

Fill in all fields accurately. Incorrect details may delay your approval.

FieldWhat to Enter
Full NameComplete name as on passport
Phone NumberWith country code — e.g. +923001234567
WhatsApp NumberIf different from your phone number
CountryYour home country
Passport NumberExactly as printed on passport
Visa NumberYour Iraq visa number
Family MembersNumber of people traveling with you

Add Family Members:

  1. Go to the "Family" section in your portal

  2. Click "Add Family Member" and enter name, relationship, passport number

Upload Documents:

  1. Go to "Documents" → upload passport copy (JPG/PDF, max 5MB)

  2. Upload visa copy and any other required documents

✈️Set Your Travel DatesStep 3▼

Under "Travel Details" in your portal:

Important: Enter accurate travel dates. Admin uses this to arrange your airport pickup. Wrong dates = no driver waiting for you.

  2. Click "Check Out" when shift ends

🏨Accommodation Management▼

Add a Hotel:

  1. Go to Admin → Accommodation → "Add Hotel"

  2. Enter hotel name, city, address, phone number

  3. Enter GPS coordinates for navigation

  4. Enter Haram distance — e.g. "800 meters from Haram"

Add Rooms:

  1. Click on a hotel → "Add Rooms"

  2. Set floor number, room type, and bed capacity

  3. Rooms are now available for pilgrim allocation

✈️Itinerary & Airport Transfers▼

  1. Go to Admin → Itinerary — see today's arrivals and departures

  2. Click any traveler with no driver assigned

  3. Click "Assign Driver" — enter driver name, phone, vehicle, meeting point, time

  4. Pilgrim automatically receives driver details via notification + email

⚠️
Nightly Alert at 20:00: System emails all admins listing every tomorrow's pilgrim with no driver assigned. You will never miss an unassigned pickup.
📿Majalis Management▼

Create a Majalis:

  1. Go to Admin → Majalis → "+ Add Majalis"

  2. Enter title, language (Urdu/English), venue, start and end time

  3. Enter Molana name, upload scholar photo → "Create"

Set the Program:

  1. Click "📋 Program" on any Majalis card

  2. Add items: Opening / Hadith / Marsiya / Salam / Noha / Closing

  3. For each item: enter time, performer name, upload performer photo

  4. Use ▲▼ to reorder → "Save Program"

🔔
Automatic: 30 min before any Majalis, system sends notification + email to all approved pilgrims with scholar photo. No manual work needed.
