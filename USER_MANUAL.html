Section 1

🕌 What Is This System?

Misbah ul Hoda (Arabic: مِصْبَاح الهُدَى — "The Lamp of Guidance") is a comprehensive, enterprise-grade digital platform purpose-built for the Arbaeen 2026 pilgrimage. It brings together every aspect of pilgrimage management — registration, accommodation, transportation, spiritual schedules, volunteer coordination, and real-time communication — into a single, beautifully designed system.

🌿 Our Mission

To serve the lovers of Imam Hussain (AS) with the utmost dignity and efficiency. We understand that Arbaeen is not simply a journey — it is an act of devotion. Misbah ul Hoda is designed to remove every logistical burden from the pilgrim's path, allowing them to focus purely on their spiritual connection.

👥

1000+ Concurrent Users

Enterprise-scale architecture supporting large groups simultaneously.

📡

Realtime Updates

Live GPS, notifications, and schedule changes via SignalR technology.

🔒

Secure & Private

JWT authentication, encrypted data, and role-based access control.

📱

Mobile First

Works seamlessly on smartphones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

💬

WhatsApp Integrated

Automatic alerts, reminders, and emergency notifications via WhatsApp.

🗺️

Google Maps GPS

Live Karwan bus tracking with stop locations and ETA displayed on map.

Section 2

🎭 User Roles

The platform supports four distinct roles. Each role has a dedicated portal with permissions tailored to their responsibilities.

Role Who Is This? Portal Key Permissions
🙏 Pilgrim Any registered pilgrim travelling for Arbaeen. Pilgrim Portal Register, view room/bus, track Karwan, view schedule, receive notifications.
🤝 Volunteer Organisation member assigned to support pilgrims on the ground. Volunteer Dashboard View tasks, check in/out, view assigned pilgrims, update attendance.
⚙️ Admin Organisation staff managing operations, rooms, schedules, and communications. Admin Panel Full operational access — approve pilgrims, allocate rooms/buses, manage Majalis, send notifications.
👑 Super Admin System owner with unrestricted access for configuration and oversight. Admin Panel + System Settings All admin permissions plus user management, role assignment, system configuration, audit logs.
💡
Tip — Which role are you?
If you are travelling as a pilgrim, go to Section 3. If you are a volunteer, see Section 4. Organisation staff should read Section 5.
Section 3

🙏 Pilgrim Guide

This section walks you through everything you need to do as a pilgrim — from first registration all the way to tracking your Karwan in Karbala.

🔑 3.1 Register & Login

Creating your account takes less than two minutes. All you need is a valid email address and a phone number.

📝 New Registration

  1. Visit the pilgrim portal or download the app.
  2. Tap Register on the home screen.
  3. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
  4. Choose a secure password (min. 8 characters).
  5. Verify your email by clicking the link sent to your inbox.
  6. Your account is created. You will be assigned the Pilgrim role automatically.

🔓 Returning Login

  1. Open the portal and tap Login.
  2. Enter your registered email and password.
  3. Tap Sign In.
  4. You will be taken directly to your personal Pilgrim Dashboard.
🔄
Forgot Password?
Tap Forgot Password on the login screen and enter your email. A reset link will be sent within seconds.

📋 3.2 Complete Your Profile

After logging in, complete your profile so the admin team can process your application. Fields marked * are required.

Field Description Required?
Full NameAs it appears on your passport.✔ Yes
Date of BirthUsed for age verification and room allocation.✔ Yes
Nationality / CountryYour country of residence or citizenship.✔ Yes
Passport NumberRequired for travel documentation.✔ Yes
Visa NumberYour Iraqi visa number if already obtained.⬤ If available
Phone NumberWhatsApp-enabled number for notifications.✔ Yes
Emergency ContactName and phone of a person to contact in emergencies.✔ Yes
Family Member CountNumber of people travelling with you (excluding yourself).✔ Yes
Medical ConditionsAny health information relevant to your care during the trip.Optional
Profile PhotoA recent clear photograph for your ID card.Optional
⚠️
Important — Name must match passport exactly
Please ensure your full name is entered exactly as it appears on your passport. Mismatches may cause delays in visa processing or airport pickup.

✈️ 3.3 Set Your Travel Dates

Enter your expected arrival and departure dates and flight details. This information is critical for the airport team.

  1. Go to your profile and open the Travel Details tab.
  2. Enter your arrival date, arrival flight number, and expected arrival time.
  3. Enter your departure date, departure flight number, and expected departure time.
  4. Select your arriving airport (Najaf International or Baghdad International).
  5. Tap Save Travel Details.
⚠️
This information is used for your airport pickup
The admin team uses your travel dates to assign drivers and airport support volunteers. The system sends an automatic nightly alert to the airport coordinator for all arrivals scheduled for the next day. Please ensure your dates and flight numbers are accurate and updated promptly if they change.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 3.4 Add Family Members

If you are travelling with family, add each member to your profile. This helps the admin allocate appropriate rooms.

  1. Go to My Profile → Family Members.
  2. Tap + Add Family Member.
  3. Enter each member's full name, date of birth, passport number, and relationship to you.
  4. Upload their passport and visa documents (see Section 3.5).
  5. Repeat for each family member.
💡
Family Room Allocation
Families are kept together in the same room or adjacent rooms wherever possible. The system uses your family count to suggest suitable room types automatically.

📎 3.5 Upload Documents

Upload clear, legible scanned copies or photographs of your travel documents. All files are stored securely and are only accessible to authorised admin staff.

Document Format Accepted Notes
Passport (Bio page)JPG, PNG, PDFMust show name, photo, and passport number clearly.
Iraqi VisaJPG, PNG, PDFUpload when obtained. You can update this later.
Passport PhotoJPG, PNGRecent photo, white or plain background.
Medical CertificatePDF, JPGRequired only for elderly or medically vulnerable pilgrims.
🔒
Your documents are protected
All uploaded files are encrypted at rest and in transit. Only authorised admin staff can view them. Files are never shared with third parties.

📊 3.6 Track Your Application Status

After submitting your profile and documents, your application is reviewed by the admin team. You can track its progress at any time from your dashboard.

Pending → Under Review → Approved  or  Rejected
Status What It Means What To Do
Pending Your application has been submitted and is waiting to be reviewed. No action needed. You will be notified when status changes.
Under Review The admin team is actively reviewing your documents and details. Keep an eye on notifications. Admin may contact you for clarification.
Approved Your application is confirmed. Room and bus allocation will follow. Check the Accommodation and Bus tabs in your dashboard.
Rejected Your application was not approved. A reason will be provided. Read the rejection reason in your notifications. Contact admin to resolve the issue and reapply.
💡
You will be notified automatically
You receive a WhatsApp message and an in-app notification every time your application status changes. You do not need to keep checking manually.

🏨 3.7 View Your Hotel & Room

Once approved and allocated, you can view all your accommodation details directly in the app.

🏨 Hotel Information

  • 🏢 Hotel name and full address
  • 📍 Distance from Haram-e-Imam Hussain (AS)
  • ⭐ Hotel rating and facilities
  • 📞 Hotel contact number
  • 🗺️ Navigate by Taxi button — opens Google Maps with hotel location pre-loaded for easy taxi navigation

🛏️ Room Details

  • 🔢 Room number and floor
  • 🏗️ Building name (if applicable)
  • 👥 Your room-mates (family members)
  • 🛏️ Bed type and count
  • 🔑 Check-in time and instructions
🚕
Navigate by Taxi
The Navigate by Taxi button on the Hotel page opens Google Maps with your hotel's exact coordinates pre-filled. Simply show this to any local driver — no need to remember the address or speak Arabic.

📿 3.8 Majalis & Schedule

The Schedule section provides a complete spiritual itinerary for your trip. It is organised into three tabs.

🎙️

Majalis Tab

Complete Majalis schedule including Urdu and English programs, Molana name, Noha Khuwan, Hadith recitation, Marsiya, and Salam. Photos of speakers are included.

🕌

Namaz Timings Tab

Daily Salah timings for your location in Karbala. Updated daily with Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha times displayed clearly.

🍽️

Food Schedule Tab

Daily meal timings for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Venue, food type, and any special dietary provisions are listed here.

🔔
Automatic 30-Minute Reminders
The system automatically sends you a reminder notification 30 minutes before each Majalis begins. You do not need to set any reminders manually.

🗺️ 3.9 Live Karwan Tracking

Know exactly where your Karwan bus is at all times using live GPS tracking integrated with Google Maps.

  1. Open the app and tap Karwan Tracking from the bottom navigation.
  2. Your assigned Karwan is displayed with its current live location on the map.
  3. View the bus's current stop, next stop, and estimated ETA.
  4. Your Karwan's Pole Number is shown prominently — use this to identify the meeting point.
  5. Tap any stop on the route to see its description and estimated arrival time.

📌 Karwan Pole Number

Each Karwan is identified by a unique Pole Number displayed on a physical sign at your bus stop and on your profile in the app. This is your meeting point. Please memorise your Pole Number before arriving.

Section 4

🤝 Volunteer Guide

Volunteers are the backbone of the pilgrimage operation. This section explains how to use your dedicated volunteer dashboard.

🔑 Login & Dashboard

Your account is created by the admin team. You will receive login credentials via WhatsApp and email before departure.

  1. Open the portal and tap Login.
  2. Enter your assigned email and password.
  3. You will be taken to the Volunteer Dashboard — a different portal from the Pilgrim portal.
  4. Your dashboard shows today's tasks, your current status, and your assigned pilgrims at a glance.

📊 Your Status

You can update your availability status so the admin team knows where to assign you.

Available Busy Offline Emergency Assigned
⚠️
Keep your status updated
The admin uses your real-time status to dispatch emergency assignments. Always update your status before going offline or when you become available again.

✅ Check In / Check Out

  1. At the start of your shift, open the app and tap Check In. Your GPS location is logged automatically.
  2. At the end of your shift, tap Check Out. Your shift hours are recorded for attendance reports.
  3. View your full attendance history in the Attendance tab of your dashboard.

📋 Tasks & Assignments

The admin assigns tasks to you directly through the system. You will receive a WhatsApp and in-app notification for each new task.

Task TypeDescription
✈️ Airport SupportMeet and assist pilgrims at the airport, assist with baggage and transfers.
🚌 Bus CoordinationEnsure all pilgrims board the correct bus at the correct Pole Number.
🏨 Hotel SupportAssist pilgrims with check-in, room keys, and any accommodation issues.
🍽️ Food DistributionOversee meal distribution at designated times and venues.
👥 Crowd ManagementManage pilgrims during Ziyarat and Majalis gatherings.
🚨 Emergency AssignmentImmediate response task assigned during urgent situations.

👥 Assigned Pilgrims

The admin may assign a specific group of pilgrims to your care. From the My Pilgrims tab, you can see each pilgrim's name, room number, contact number, and any special requirements. This allows you to provide personalised support.

Section 5

⚙️ Admin Guide

The Admin Panel is a powerful management hub for organisation staff. This section covers all administrative functions in detail.

📊 5.1 Dashboard Overview

The admin dashboard provides a real-time overview of the entire operation.

👥

Pilgrim Summary

Total pilgrims, breakdown by status (Pending / Approved / Rejected), and today's arrivals/departures.

🏨

Accommodation Overview

Total rooms, occupied vs available, and occupancy percentage per hotel.

🚌

Bus & Karwan Status

Active buses, current locations, and departure schedules for the day.

🤝

Volunteer Activity

Number of volunteers available, busy, and offline in real time.

📅

Today's Schedule

Majalis, meals, and tours scheduled for today with time and venue.

🔔

Recent Notifications

Last 10 notifications sent with delivery status (Sent / Delivered / Read).

🙏 5.2 Pilgrim Management

Manage all pilgrim applications from a single list view with powerful search, filter, and bulk actions.

Reviewing Applications

  1. Go to Pilgrims → All Applications.
  2. Use filters to find applications by status, date, or name.
  3. Click a pilgrim's name to view their full profile and documents.
  4. Tap Approve or Reject. If rejecting, enter a reason — this is sent to the pilgrim automatically.

Allocating Room & Bus

  1. Open an approved pilgrim's profile.
  2. Tap Allocate Room — a list of available rooms in available hotels is shown.
  3. Select the appropriate room and confirm. The pilgrim is notified instantly.
  4. Tap Allocate Bus and assign the pilgrim to their Karwan group.
💡
Smart Family Grouping
The system automatically groups family members together when allocating rooms. If a suitable room for the full family is not available, it will suggest the closest adjacent rooms.

🏨 5.3 Accommodation Management

Configure hotels, buildings, floors, and rooms before pilgrims arrive.

Adding a Hotel

  1. Go to Accommodation → Hotels → Add Hotel.
  2. Enter hotel name, full address, star rating, and contact number.
  3. Enter the distance from Haram-e-Imam Hussain (AS) in metres.
  4. Add the hotel's GPS coordinates for the Navigate by Taxi feature.
  5. Tap Save Hotel.

Adding Rooms

  1. Open the hotel and go to Buildings → Floors → Rooms.
  2. Tap Add Room. Enter room number, floor, room type, and bed count.
  3. Set maximum occupancy and whether the room is suitable for families.
  4. Rooms are now visible in the allocation screen.

✈️ 5.4 Itinerary & Airport Transfers

Manage airport pickups and track incoming pilgrim arrivals efficiently.

  1. Go to Itinerary → Airport Transfers.
  2. The system automatically groups all pilgrims arriving on the same day and flight.
  3. Tap a group to assign a driver from the available drivers list.
  4. Enter the driver's name, vehicle number, and contact number.
  5. Tap Assign — the pilgrim receives an automatic notification with driver details.
⚠️
Automatic Nightly Airport Alert
Every night at 10:00 PM, the system automatically sends a summary alert to the Airport Coordinator and all assigned drivers listing all pilgrim arrivals scheduled for the following day. This ensures no one is missed. No manual action is needed.

🎙️ 5.5 Majalis Management

Create and manage the full Majalis program for the trip.

Adding a Majalis

  1. Go to Majalis → Add Majalis.
  2. Enter the title, date, time, venue, and language (Urdu / English).
  3. Add the Molana profile — name, photo, and short biography.
  4. Add the Noha Khuwan profile — name and photo.
  5. Set the program order — select from: Hadith Recitation, Marsiya, Salam, Noha, Main Majalis, Dua.
  6. Upload any promotional photos or banners for the event.
  7. Tap Save & Publish — the event is immediately visible to all pilgrims.
🔔
Automatic 30-Minute Reminder
The system automatically sends a reminder to all pilgrims 30 minutes before each Majalis. The reminder includes the Molana name, venue, and a direct link to view the program. You do not need to send this manually.

🗺️ 5.6 Tours

Organise Ziyarat tours and group excursions with day-by-day schedules.

  1. Go to Tours → Create Tour.
  2. Enter tour name, description, start date, and end date.
  3. Add the Day Schedule — for each day, add locations, departure times, and return times.
  4. Assign pilgrims or groups to the tour.
  5. Enable Automatic Day Reminder — the system sends a reminder at 8:00 AM on the day of each tour stop.
  6. Tap Publish Tour — pilgrims can see the full tour schedule in their app.

🤝 5.7 Volunteer Management

Manage your entire volunteer team from registration to real-time dispatch.

Adding Volunteers

  • ➕ Go to Volunteers → Add Volunteer
  • 📧 Enter name, email, phone, and assigned role
  • 🔑 System sends login credentials via WhatsApp
  • 📋 Assign the volunteer to a department (Airport, Hotels, Buses, etc.)

Assigning Tasks

  • 📋 Go to Tasks → Assign Task
  • 👤 Select a volunteer or broadcast to a department
  • 📝 Enter task description, priority, and due time
  • 📲 Volunteer receives WhatsApp notification immediately
💡
Emergency Dispatch
In an emergency, use Emergency Assign to instantly dispatch all Available volunteers to a location. They receive an urgent WhatsApp alert and their status changes to Emergency Assigned automatically.

FeatureDescription

👤 Individual MessageSend a private notification to a specific pilgrim (e.g., room change, personal message).

📊 Delivery ReportsView read receipts and delivery status for each notification sent.

💰 5.9 Finance

Track all financial records related to the pilgrimage package and expenses.

💵

Pilgrim Payments

🧾

Expense Tracking

Log hotel costs, bus hire, Majalis expenses, and other operational costs.

📈

Financial Summary

📧

Payment Receipts

📊 5.10 Reports & Export

Generate comprehensive reports for operations, finance, and compliance purposes.

  • 📋 Pilgrim List Report — Full list of all pilgrims with contact details, room, and bus allocation.
  • 🏨 Occupancy Report — Room-by-room occupancy across all hotels.
  • ✈️ Arrivals & Departures Report — Daily airport transfer schedule.
  • 🤝 Volunteer Attendance Report — Shift hours and attendance for each volunteer.
  • 💰 Financial Report — Full income and expense summary.
  • 🔔 Notification Log — Complete history of all notifications sent.
  • 📁 Export to Excel/PDF — All reports can be exported for offline use or printing.
💡
Audit Log
Every action taken by any admin or super admin is recorded in the Audit Log with the user's name, timestamp, and description. This provides full accountability and traceability for all system actions.
