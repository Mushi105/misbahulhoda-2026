## 1. What is this System?

**Misbah ul Hoda — Arbaeen 2026** is a complete digital management platform for pilgrims traveling to Karbala for Arbaeen. Everything that was previously done on paper, WhatsApp groups, or phone calls is now managed in one place:

- Pilgrim registration and application status
- Hotel and room allocation
- Airport pickup and drop-off coordination
- Majalis schedule with full program (Hadith, Marsiya, Salam, Noha)
- Namaz timings and food schedule
- Live Karwan GPS tracking
- Tour management with day-by-day schedule
- Volunteer coordination
- Notifications and announcements
- Finance and budget tracking

---

## 4. Pilgrim Guide

### 3.1 How to Register

1. Go to the website and click **"Pilgrim Portal"**
2. Click **"Register"** — enter your full name, email, and password
3. Log in with your new account

### 3.2 Complete Your Profile

After logging in, fill in your profile:

| Field | What to Enter |
|---|---|
| Full Name | Your complete name |
| Phone Number | With country code (e.g. +923001234567) |
| WhatsApp Number | If different from phone |
| Country | Your country |
| Passport Number | As shown on your passport |
| Visa Number | Your Iraq visa number |
| Family Members | Number of people traveling with you |

Click **"Save Profile"** — your application is now **Pending** review.

### 3.3 Set Your Travel Dates

Under **"Travel Details"**, enter:
- **Arrival date & time** — when you land in Iraq
- **Arrival flight number** — e.g. PK-745
- **Arrival airport** — e.g. Baghdad International
- **Departure date & time** — when you fly back
- **Departure flight number**

> ⚠️ **Important:** Enter accurate travel dates. The admin team uses this to arrange your airport pickup and prepare the reception team.

### 3.4 Add Family Members

If family members are traveling with you:
1. Go to the **"Family"** section in your portal
2. Click **"Add Family Member"**
3. Enter their name, relationship, and passport number

### 3.5 Upload Documents

Under **"Documents"**:
- Upload your **passport copy** (JPG or PDF, max 5MB)
- Upload your **visa copy**
- Any other required documents

### 3.6 Track Your Application Status

Your application goes through these stages:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟡 **Pending** | Submitted, waiting for admin review |
| 🔵 **Under Review** | Admin is reviewing your documents |
| ✅ **Approved** | You are confirmed for Arbaeen 2026 |
| ❌ **Rejected** | Application not accepted (reason will be shown) |

You will receive an **email and app notification** when your status changes.

### 3.7 View Your Hotel & Room

Once approved and a room is assigned:
- Your **hotel name** will appear in your portal
- **Distance from Haram Imam Hussain (AS)** is shown
- Tap **"Navigate to Hotel"** — opens Google Maps for taxi directions
- Tap **"Navigate to Haram"** — walking directions to Haram Imam Hussain (AS)

### 3.8 View Your Bus

Your allocated bus number and Karwan assignment will appear in your portal once assigned by admin.

### 3.9 Majalis & Schedule

Under **"Majalis & Schedule"** you can see:

**Majalis Tab:**
- Today's Majalis with scholar photo
- Full program timeline: Hadith → Marsiya → Salam → Noha
- Performer names and photos
- Venue and time

**Namaz Timings Tab:**
- Today's prayer times (Fajr, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha)
- Location for each prayer

**Food Schedule Tab:**
- Meal times (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Sehri, Iftaar)
- Location and estimated servings

### 3.10 Live Karwan Tracking

Under **"Live Tracking"**:
1. Select your Karwan from the list
2. Live GPS map shows the Karwan's current location
3. Map updates in real-time

### 3.11 Tours

If a Ziyarat tour is offered:
1. Go to **"Available Tours"**
2. Read the tour details and day-by-day schedule
3. Select a package and apply
4. You will be notified when the tour date is approaching

### 3.12 Notifications

You will receive automatic notifications for:
- Application status change (approved/rejected)
- Room allocation
- Majalis starting in 30 minutes (email + app)
- Travel reminders (2 days before your flight)
- Final reminder (5 hours before your flight)
- Airport pickup driver details

## 5. Volunteer Guide

### 4.1 How to Log In

- Open the website and click **"Volunteer Portal"**
- Log in with your volunteer credentials (provided by admin)

### 4.2 Your Dashboard

Your dashboard shows:
- Your current status (Available / Busy / Offline)
- Assigned tasks
- Assigned zone
- Pilgrims assigned to you
- Attendance history

### 4.3 Check In / Check Out

- Click **"Check In"** when you start your shift
- Click **"Check Out"** when your shift ends
- Attendance is automatically recorded

### 4.4 Your Tasks

Each task shows:
- Task description
- Priority (Low / Medium / High / Emergency)
- Deadline
- Status

Click **"Mark Complete"** when a task is done.

### 4.5 Assigned Pilgrims

If pilgrims are assigned to you:
- Their name, phone, hotel, and flight details are shown
- You are responsible for their airport support or hotel coordination

## 6. Admin Guide

### 5.1 Dashboard

The admin dashboard gives a real-time overview:
- Total pilgrims (pending / approved / rejected)
- Volunteers (available / busy / offline)
- Accommodation occupancy rate
- Today's arrivals and departures with flight details
- Pilgrims without drivers assigned (flagged in red)
- Next 3-day travel schedule

### 5.2 Managing Pilgrims

Go to **Admin → Pilgrims**:

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| View all pilgrims | Search by name, filter by status |
| Approve a pilgrim | Click pilgrim → "Approve" |
| Reject a pilgrim | Click pilgrim → "Reject" → enter reason |
| Mark Under Review | Click pilgrim → "Under Review" |
| Allocate Room | Click pilgrim → "Allocate Room" → select room |
| Allocate Bus | Click pilgrim → "Allocate Bus" → select bus |

When you approve a pilgrim, they automatically receive an email and notification.

### 5.3 Accommodation

Go to **Admin → Accommodation**:

**Add a Hotel:**
1. Click **"Add Hotel"**
2. Enter hotel name, city, address, phone
3. Enter GPS coordinates (latitude/longitude) for navigation
4. Enter Haram distance (e.g. "800 meters from Haram") — pilgrims see this
5. Click **"Save"**

**Add Rooms:**
1. Click on a hotel → **"Add Rooms"**
2. Set floor number, room type, bed capacity
3. Rooms are now available for allocation

### 5.4 Itinerary (Airport Transfers)

Go to **Admin → Itinerary**:

**View today's arrivals and departures** — all pilgrims flying today are listed with:
- Flight number, time, airport
- Transfer status (driver assigned or not)

**Assign a Driver:**
1. Click a pilgrim's travel entry
2. Click **"Assign Driver"**
3. Enter driver name, phone, vehicle type, meeting point, scheduled time
4. Pilgrim automatically receives the driver details via notification + email

> ⚠️ Every evening at 20:00, the system automatically alerts you if any tomorrow's traveler has no driver assigned.

### 5.5 Majalis Management

Go to **Admin → Majalis**:

**Add a Majalis:**
1. Click **"+ Add Majalis"**
2. Enter title, language (Urdu/English), venue, start/end time
3. Enter Molana name and Noha Khuwan name
4. Upload scholar photo (from device or paste URL)
5. Click **"Create"**

**Set the Program (Hadith, Marsiya, Salam, Noha):**
1. Click **"📋 Program"** on any Majalis card
2. Add program items in order:
   - Select type: Opening / Hadith / Marsiya / Salam / Noha / Closing / Break
   - Enter time (e.g. 08:15)
   - Enter performer name
   - Upload performer photo
3. Reorder items using ▲▼ buttons
4. Click **"Save Program"**

**Namaz Timings:**
1. Click **"Set Namaz Timings"**
2. Select date
3. Enter time and venue for each prayer (Fajr, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha)
4. Click **"Save"**

**Food Schedule:**
1. Click **"+ Add Food Schedule"**
2. Select meal type (Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner / Sehri / Iftaar)
3. Enter date & time, location, description, estimated servings
4. Click **"Add"**

> 🔔 **Automatic Reminder:** 30 minutes before any Majalis starts, the system automatically sends a notification + email to all approved pilgrims with the scholar's photo.

### 5.6 Tours

Go to **Admin → Tours**:

**Create a Tour:**
1. Click **"+ New Tour"**
2. Enter tour name, destination, start/end dates, max participants
3. Add packages (e.g. Economy / Standard / Premium with different prices)
4. Set status to **"Open"** to allow pilgrims to apply

**Set Day Schedule:**
1. Open a tour → **"Setup Schedule"**
2. Fill in each day: date, city, country, hotel, activities
3. Mark key days (e.g. Arbaeen day) with ⭐
4. Click **"Save Schedule"**

**Send Reminder to Pilgrims:**
- Click **"Send Reminder"** — all approved pilgrims receive notification + email about upcoming tour

**Live Tour Monitor:**
- Click **"Tour Monitor"** to see real-time tour status, day schedule, and pilgrim feedback

### 5.7 Volunteers

Go to **Admin → Volunteers**:
- Add volunteers (name, skills, zone)
- Assign tasks with priority and deadline
- Track check-in/check-out attendance
- View live status (Available / Busy / Offline)
- Assign pilgrims to specific volunteers for support

### 5.8 Notifications

Go to **Admin → Notifications**:
- Send a notification to a specific user
- Broadcast to all pilgrims, all volunteers, or everyone
- Send emergency alerts (red banner, high priority)

### 5.9 Notice Board

Go to **Admin → Notice Board**:
- Post announcements visible to all pilgrims
- Set expiry date for time-sensitive notices
- Pilgrims see the notice board on their dashboard

### 5.10 Finance

Go to **Admin → Finance**:
- Track expenses by category (Transport / Food / Accommodation / etc.)
- Set budgets and see budget vs. actual
- Manage staff payments and mark as paid
- Record travel tickets
- Export full finance report

Go to **Admin → International Finance**:
- Record incoming payments from pilgrims
- Manage vendor contracts (hotels, bus companies)
- Track money transfers to Iraq

### 5.11 Users

Go to **Admin → Users**:
- Create new admin/volunteer/staff accounts
- Activate or deactivate accounts
- Change user roles

### 5.12 Reports

Go to **Admin → Reports**:
- Export full pilgrim list (Excel/CSV)
- Export volunteer list
- View summary statistics
