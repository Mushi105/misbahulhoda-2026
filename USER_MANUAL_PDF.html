📖What is Misbah ul Hoda?

Misbah ul Hoda — Arbaeen 2026 is a complete digital management platform for pilgrims traveling to Karbala. Everything previously done on paper, WhatsApp groups, or phone calls is now managed in one professional system — accessible from any device, anywhere in the world.

🕌
Pilgrim Registration
Online application, document upload, status tracking
🏨

✈️
Airport Pickup
Driver assignment with automatic notifications
📿
Majalis Schedule
Full program with scholar photos and timings
📍
Live Karwan Tracking
Real-time GPS map of your Karwan bus
🔔
Auto Notifications
Email + app alerts for every key event
🗓️
Ziyarat Tours
Day-by-day tour schedule and applications
🤝
Volunteer System
Task management, attendance, zone coordination
💰
Finance Tracking
Budget, expenses, international payments
👥User Roles
RoleWhoWhat They Can Do
🕌 PilgrimRegistered pilgrimsApply for Arbaeen, submit travel details, track status, view hotel/room, see Majalis schedule, track Karwan live on map
🤝 VolunteerGround support staffView assigned tasks, check in/out for attendance, coordinate pilgrim support, receive emergency alerts
⚙️ AdminOffice managersApprove/reject pilgrims, allocate rooms and buses, manage Majalis, coordinate airport transfers, send notifications

Registration & Profile
Section 1 of 3
🕌
Pilgrim Guide
Follow these steps in order to complete your registration and get approved for Arbaeen 2026. Each step is required before proceeding to the next.
📝Step 1 — Register & Log InStart Here

  1. Open the website and click "Pilgrim Portal" on the homepage

  2. Click "Register" — enter your full name, email address, and choose a password

  3. Click "Create Account" — you will be logged in automatically

  4. You will land on your personal Pilgrim Dashboard — this is your home screen

ℹ️
If you already have an account, simply click "Sign In" and enter your email and password. Forgot password? Click "Forgot Password" and a reset link will be sent to your email.
👤Step 2 — Complete Your ProfileRequired

After logging in, go to Profile and fill in all information. Incomplete or incorrect details may delay your approval.

Adding Family Members

  1. Go to the "Family" section in your portal

  2. Click "Add Family Member"

  3. Enter their: full name, relationship (Spouse/Child/Parent), and passport number

  4. Repeat for each family member traveling with you

Uploading Documents

  1. Go to "Documents" in the left menu

  2. Click "Upload Passport" — select a clear photo or scanned PDF (max 5MB)

  3. Click "Upload Visa" — upload your Iraq visa copy

  4. Wait for the green checkmark ✅ confirming the upload was successful

Important: Documents must be clearly readable. Blurry or cropped images will be rejected. Accepted formats: JPG, PNG, PDF. Maximum file size: 5MB per file.

Majalis, Tracking & Tours
Section 1 of 3
📿Majalis, Namaz Timings & Food Schedule

Go to "Majalis" from the navigation menu. You will see three tabs:

📿 Majalis Tab — Daily Program

  • Today's Majalis with the scholar's photo
  • Full program timeline: Opening → Hadith → Marsiya → Salam → Noha → Closing
  • Each item shows: start time, performer name, and performer photo
  • Venue name and address
  • Language (Urdu / English)

🕌 Namaz Timings Tab

  • Today's prayer times: Fajr, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha
  • Location for Jamaat prayers

🍽️ Food Schedule Tab

  • Meal times: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Sehri, Iftaar (during Ramadan)
  • Location and estimated number of servings
🔔
Automatic Reminder: You receive a push notification and email 30 minutes before every Majalis — with the scholar's photo, time, and venue. You will never miss a Majalis.
📍Live Karwan Tracking

  1. Go to "Live Tracking" from the navigation menu

  2. You will see a list of all active Karwans — select your Karwan

  3. A live GPS map opens showing the exact current location of your Karwan bus

  4. The map updates automatically in real-time — no need to refresh

  5. Tap any bus icon to see: driver name, bus number, next stop, and ETA

🗓️Ziyarat Tours

If Ziyarat tours are available, you can browse and apply:

  1. Go to "Tours" → "Available Tours" from the menu

  2. Each tour shows: destination, total days, day-by-day schedule, and package price

  3. Click a tour → "View Details" to see the complete daily itinerary

  4. Select a package (Economy / Standard / Premium) and click "Apply"

  5. Your application is sent to admin — you will be notified of approval

  6. As the tour date approaches, the system automatically sends you reminder notifications

ℹ️
Tour places are limited. Apply early to secure your spot. Once a tour is full, the status changes to "Full" and no more applications are accepted.

Section 3 of 3
🏨Accommodation Management

Go to Admin → Accommodation. This section manages all hotels, buildings, floors, and rooms.

Adding a New Hotel

  1. Click "+ Add Hotel"

  2. Enter: hotel name, city (Karbala / Najaf / Baghdad), full address, phone number

  3. Enter GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude) — pilgrims use this to navigate from airport

  4. Enter Haram distance text — e.g. "800 meters walking from Haram Imam Hussain (AS)" — this is shown to pilgrims

  5. Click "Save Hotel"

Adding Rooms to a Hotel

  1. Click on any hotel card → click "Add Rooms" or "Manage Rooms"

  2. Set: floor number, room number, room type (Single / Double / Family / Suite), bed capacity

  3. Set current status: Available / Occupied / Maintenance

  4. Click "Save" — room is now available for pilgrim allocation

ℹ️
Rooms are automatically marked "Occupied" when a pilgrim is allocated to them, and return to "Available" when deallocated. The occupancy rate on the dashboard updates in real-time.
✈️Itinerary & Airport Transfer Management

Go to Admin → Itinerary. This page shows all arrivals and departures, organized by date.

ColumnWhat it Shows
Pilgrim NameName, phone, and link to their full profile
Flight DetailsFlight number, arrival time, airport name
Transfer Status🟢 Driver Assigned / 🔴 No Driver (highlighted red)
Driver DetailsDriver name, phone, vehicle if assigned
Assigning a Driver

  1. Click on a pilgrim row that shows "No Driver" (red)

  2. Click "Assign Driver"

  3. Enter: driver full name, driver phone number, vehicle type (e.g. Toyota Corolla), meeting point (e.g. Arrivals Hall Gate 3), scheduled pickup time

  4. Click "Confirm Assignment"

  5. The pilgrim automatically receives all driver details via notification + email immediately

⚠️
Nightly Auto-Alert at 20:00: Every evening, the system automatically emails ALL admins with a list of every pilgrim arriving the next day who has no driver assigned. This ensures no pilgrim is left unattended at the airport.

Section 3 of 3
📿Majalis Management

Go to Admin → Majalis. This section manages the full Majalis schedule that pilgrims see in their portal.

Creating a Majalis

  1. Click "+ Add Majalis"

  2. Enter: title, language (Urdu / English), venue name and address

  3. Set: start time and end time

  4. Enter: Molana / Scholar name

  5. Upload scholar photo (JPG/PNG, clear face photo) — appears in notifications sent to pilgrims

  6. Click "Create Majalis"

Setting the Full Program

  1. Click "📋 Program" button on any Majalis card

  2. Click "+ Add Item" — select type: Opening / Hadith / Marsiya / Salam / Noha / Closing / Break

  3. For each item enter: time (e.g. 08:15), performer name, and upload their photo

  4. Continue adding all items in chronological order

  5. Use the ▲▼ arrows to reorder items if needed

  6. Click "Save Program" — pilgrims can now see the full detailed program

Namaz Timings & Food Schedule

Within the Majalis section, you can also manage:

  • Click "Set Namaz Timings" → select date → enter time and venue for each of the 5 prayers
  • Click "+ Add Food Schedule" → select meal type → enter date, time, location, and estimated servings
🔔
Automatic: 30 minutes before any Majalis starts, the system automatically sends a push notification + email to all approved pilgrims — including the scholar's photo, exact time, and venue. No manual action needed.
